Method and limits

Each run creates both environments afresh. Import, texture readiness, shader preparation and first submitted render are logged separately and excluded from steady timing. Browser/driver caches can remain warm. Each block has a 60-second blank idle recovery, 30 warmup frames and a finite measured frame count. Recovery does not establish temperature or clock parity. Timed rendering uses the same frozen time and camera, excluding spacecraft, AO and application UI. GPU queries sample every fifth frame; disjoint/missing values are not zero. Hidden page, resize or lost context invalidates the run. Repeat in balanced orders. The report requires repeated baseline and candidate block means within a 5% range and a difference larger than that observed variation; otherwise timing remains inconclusive. This heuristic is not a confidence interval. No power, heat or battery claims follow from this fixture.
