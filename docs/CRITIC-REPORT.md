## Findings resolved during review

The initial dropdown put its links before the toggle in DOM order, causing forward Tab to skip them after opening. Its order is corrected. Selecting the current destination now returns focus to the toggle, and an arriving flight preserves an open navigation disclosure. The corresponding [browser records](/Users/mikaelvincent/Documents/GitHub/orbital-folio/docs/evidence/open-walkway-revision/browser-qa.json) demonstrate these cases.

The first doorway implementation recentered the camera during hover, while the geometry audit required bounded yaw to see neighboring architecture. Focus/hover now produces the matching small viewing angle. The [focused doorway capture](/Users/mikaelvincent/Documents/GitHub/orbital-folio/docs/evidence/open-walkway-revision/doorway-peek.jpg) and [sightline audit](/Users/mikaelvincent/Documents/GitHub/orbital-folio/docs/evidence/open-walkway-revision/neighbor-views.json) support a narrow wall/threshold glimpse, not a full view into the next room. No portfolio exploit was observed. The final resolved dependency tree and independent registry audit confirm the patched version and zero advisories; the pre-patch findings are retained. The toolchain was not downgraded. ## Performance interpretation

The slow production overview sample records approximately 50ms median / 66.7ms p95 at 1440×1000 and effective DPR1. The settled Projects sample records 49.1/66.7ms, with 85 of 360 intervals above 50ms. These are material observations, not dismissed as startup or an old-DPR mistake.

Later unchanged production repeats record overview 16.7/18.6ms and Projects 16.7/18.5ms, both with zero intervals above 50ms. Final phone/tablet records are also faster. The [renderer-absent control](/Users/mikaelvincent/Documents/GitHub/orbital-folio/docs/evidence/open-walkway-revision/renderer-absent-control.json) records 180 visible callbacks over approximately 3.01 seconds at DPR1, 16.7ms median / 18.6ms p95, with no canvas in Reading view. This distinguishes that control from a paused live renderer; it does not isolate GPU, shader or draw-call cost. The cause of the slow/fast variability remains unresolved.

The scene records roughly 210 draw calls and 0.54–0.58 million triangles in inspected states. The optional Three chunk is approximately 182.5KB gzip; the model is 18.5KB and environment 6.4KB. Recorded HTML is approximately 12.8–13.5KB computed gzip, with warm loopback full responses approximately 11.3–17.2ms. These [measurements](/Users/mikaelvincent/Documents/GitHub/orbital-folio/docs/evidence/open-walkway-revision/production-measurements.json) are not public-network, physical-device or field CWV measurements.

Clouds use a filtered procedural gradient volume and require no external imagery. The [cloud review](/Users/mikaelvincent/Documents/GitHub/orbital-folio/docs/evidence/open-walkway-revision/cloud-review.md) explicitly records higher cross-resolution alpha error in its CPU comparison. Finer structure is visible, but neither eliminated shimmer nor a GPU performance improvement is proven. That limitation remains part of acceptance.
