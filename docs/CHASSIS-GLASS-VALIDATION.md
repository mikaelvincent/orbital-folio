# Chassis closures and overview glass

The revised ladder liner has a curved wall-to-back join and no separate fan-shaped end sheets. Its lower curved strip now closes at the correct tangent. The shared outer outline joins the ladder to the roof and keel without a short raised step, and the shoulder depth caps follow that outline.

The cabin floors and ceilings now close against the front aperture instead of exposing empty blue strips. The walking surface and furnishing placements remain fixed. The independent [actual-mesh audit](evidence/chassis-glass/cabin-closure-critic-audit.json) passes 324 targeted rays across wide and compact layouts: 144 closure checks, 24 walking-height checks, 108 passage-clearance checks and 48 ladder-wall checks. Run the [audit script](evidence/chassis-glass/cabin-closure-critic-audit.mjs) with Node from the repository root; its optional arguments are repository path and output path.

Overview labels, Home, navigation, Reading view and status now share translucent cool glass, fine borders and lighter typography. The portfolio heading keeps its appearance and travel behavior. Actual measured label/heading sizes are 14/18px at 320px width, 14/18.72px at 390px, 22.24/32px at 1920px and 24/32px at 2560px. All recorded callouts have at least 44px height. Full Overview and Reading view text fits the 320px screen after adjusting mobile spacing.

[Hashes](evidence/chassis-glass/source-hashes.json) identify the reviewed source. The GPU captures use the development server. No production-runtime, physical-device, exhaustive accessibility or FPS claim is made. Deferred readers, forms and admin remain outside this iteration. Tradeoffs: the filled lower reveals are visually heavier, but make the cutaway read as a solid structure. Strong downward camera angles can hide part of a physical interior header; exterior overview labels remain legible. The compact landscape craft retains its existing small framing. The repairs add simple geometry and shared CSS tokens without new dependencies.
