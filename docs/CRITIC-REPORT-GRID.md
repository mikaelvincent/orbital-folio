## Evidence that closes the earlier findings

The last retarget carries nonzero velocity instead of resetting it; the camera initially continues its existing motion while decelerating toward the new destination. Controller tests verify velocity/acceleration bounds and convergence at 30/60/120Hz. The trace supplements those tests with actual renderer output, rather than only testing the standalone interpolation function.

The strong non-neutral pause records preserve camera position `-5.1131,3.9565,18.0363`, quaternion `-0.102460,-0.139063,-0.014467,0.984862` and hover offset `0.03668,-0.03721`. Both frozen samples and the first resume sample preserve those outputs and active time 31.462. Source retains the rendered pointer, hover and dolly contributions while paused and smoothly targets them to zero when reading begins. This closes the previously identified snap caused by bypassing retained state.

**Physical reader alignment.** The original Atlas mismatch was caused by focus scrolling the CSS3D projection root. `overflow: clip` prevents that root from becoming a scroll container. Corrected desktop DOM bounds agree with projected physical bounds to approximately 0.002px in the recorded sample. Mobile dossier/contact evidence likewise shows projection scroll zero and aligned paper. The corrected Atlas screenshot has neither the previous displaced content nor a blank strip in the physical frame.

**Capacity and labels.** The model audit covers empty, single, nine and twelve project configurations, isolated compartment hover, simple ray targets and room enclosure geometry. Browser evidence covers the tenth project through its full second-page/open/return flow. Final 320px screenshots verify the pagination and label-collision fixes. Private long-label preview demonstrates data-driven labels without scene edits; its restoration record reports public content intact and the private draft restored. Long names can intentionally ellipsize, while the navigation/title/accessibility text retains the full value.

**Loader and fallback.** The loader screenshot uses the documented development-only four-second import gate, not a fabricated progress percentage or a production delay. Recorded completion produces the renderer; choosing Reading view during the gate remains in reading mode after the delay, without creating a renderer. The initial `#room-reader` choice is honored during hydration. A hidden loader node can remain under the hidden render container; DOM presence is not visibility. Fresh post-WebGL-loss evidence includes a matching zero-violation accessibility run and readable content. Failed initialization and unavailable graphics do not strand the visitor behind the scene.

## Remaining non-blocking limits

The ship's lighting and surface detail can still become richer. Tablet and long custom labels sometimes ellipsize even when other navigation remains fully readable. These are polish deductions, not missing content or broken navigation.

Frame intervals are measurements on the available browser/runtime. Debug traces contain substantial outliers, while clean final steady-state samples are smooth. Their cause cannot be isolated from these artifacts alone. No claim is made about sustained low-end-phone thermal behavior, GPU timing, every browser engine, real assistive technology or field Core Web Vitals. Continued manual device testing would strengthen confidence.

Remote Sites hosting remains blocked by the previously documented SIWC callback-registration HTTP 409 incident. It is separate from this local revision and remains unresolved. No public deployment or authorization bypass is claimed. Production must preserve the documented trusted-gateway identity boundary. All fictional history remains visibly marked sample/concept until the owner replaces and publishes real content through the admin.
