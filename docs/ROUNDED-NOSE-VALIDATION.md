# Rounded ladder bay, paired signs and two-level lighting

This render-only iteration uses the temporary **75/100 critic target and 6/10 minimum per area**. The separate readers, forms, admin workflows and unchanged Earth/background algorithms remain outside scope. The previous [portrait review](CRITIC-REPORT-PORTRAIT-LADDER.md) is historical; the fresh result is in [CRITIC-REPORT.md](CRITIC-REPORT.md).

## Changes

The ladder bay now has long elliptical shoulders, measuring 1.36 units across ×2.14 units high in compact model space, rather than the former .74-unit corner rounding. They cover about 67% of the bay height and continue through the full shell depth. A 2.12-unit straight center preserves the docking opening. The ladder is narrower and shifts inward; the lower landing narrows to fit the taper. Wide layout scales horizontal dimensions while retaining the same curve and open side entries.

Each indoor destination plaque has matching physical symbols on both sides of its centered text. Ordinary doors now use upward arrows for “continue through.” Ladder arrows retain the actual up/down deck direction, accompanied by a ladder pictogram. The previously repaired backing/enamel separation and matte ink remain intact.

Wayfinding guidance depends on context. [Canada’s Federal Identity Program, section 4.3](https://www.canada.ca/en/treasury-board-secretariat/services/government-communications/federal-identity-program/manual.html) allows down-arrows on overhead straight-ahead signs; [PANYNJ’s 2024 manual, section 6.2.4](https://wayfinding.panynj.gov/pa-cms/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/PANYNJWayfindingManual-Print-1point2.pdf) normally uses up-arrows for straight ahead and distinguishes vertical circulation with pictograms. The new convention is a consistent design choice for this scene, not a claim that the old overhead arrows violated a universal standard.

All interiors now use .50 by default and 1.00 for hover, settled selection or actual transit. These are material multipliers, not measured screen luminance. Ladder hover still activates from inside a room, as previously requested. Exterior materials stay at full brightness, and no extra doorway or ladder lights were added. Database content, URLs and the camera implementation are unchanged.
