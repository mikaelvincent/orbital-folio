# Vent replacement audit

Baseline: `453bf12`. The retained exception is exactly **eight cabin air returns**:
two beside each room heading. Their positions, mounting and louvre geometry are
unchanged. The other replacements use rounded solid faces, seated edges and
captive hardware in the existing cream, navy, alloy and restrained amber palette.
These are artistic cues to plausible equipment functions, not engineering claims.

control or concealed rear decoration. Existing solid docking-side cassettes remain.

## Functional patterns deliberately retained

- The notebook/library cage shows and restrains books; its bound frame and zipper
  distinguish it from ventilation. The blanket cage was replaced because its
  warmer insert and dense mesh read more strongly as a heater at room distance.
- Archive rack mounting/index holes, drawer rails, tool grip ribs, zippers,
  keyboards, headset details and radio meter segments keep their identifiable
  mechanical or interface functions. Repeated detail alone was not classified as
  a vent.
- The microphone grille could function as acoustic hardware. A smooth windscreen
  preserves that role while removing the grille appearance entirely.
- Door iris blades, ladder rungs/rails, lamps, conduit packs and existing isolation
  controls remain. No screen content, social link or interactive destination was
  added or removed by the equipment redesign.

## Geometry and review evidence

Cabin checks cover both layouts, actual wall seating, furniture bounds, passive
picking and unobstructed headers/screens. The new cabin audit regression permits
vent/grille/louvre/intake parts only inside the two preserved label-side returns.
The main furniture bounding boxes are unchanged in wide and compact layouts.

The first broad crown plate followed the boundary but bridged the curved liner
between its edges. Subdividing along the curved axis corrected that gap. The
saved crown-fit audit checks 320 backing-face centers, embedded by approximately
0.00149–0.00200 model units. Exterior visibility rays check real scene triangles
at the supported overview/drag poses: both roof covers are exposed in the tested
default views; shoulder covers are visible from the ordinary landscape side,
with no claim that they are visible from the opposite portrait side.

The combined [geometry inventory](geometry-inventory.json) records **419 → 416
visible meshes, 895,624 → 896,518 triangles and +63,164 geometry-array bytes**.
This is a static inventory, not a GPU timing or total-memory measurement. New
fixtures use existing material palettes with no new texture, light or ongoing
animation. No held optimization candidate was enabled.
