# Third cloud candidate: actual GPU review

Inspected `docs/evidence/natural-orbit-revision/overview-cloud-final-first.jpg`, reported 1440×1000 and approximately 14 active seconds. This is the first actual GPU capture reviewed for the third candidate; it supersedes the prior statement that this candidate had only CPU-preview evidence. The displayed development audit overlay is not part of the visual judgment.

**Cloud appearance still does not pass the requested realistic, high-fidelity target.** The broad front and clear-ocean region are visibly better organized than the second candidate's repeated foam. However, the foreground now reads as stretched, blurred brush strokes. The left patches are especially soft and smeared; the right front has a little more internal structure but still lacks fine irregular erosion and convincing lit/shaded billows. The outer limb is coherent and smoother. This is an improvement in weather organization, not yet an adequate realism result.

The next review should specifically look for irregular fine structure at the foreground scale, distinguish naturally wispy filaments from broad filtering blur, and verify directional relief without introducing dark contour outlines or returning to uniform holes. Diagnose actual texture/mip/filter and density-remap behavior at the rendered distance. Merely increasing white coverage, naming more cloud types or increasing a nominal texture dimension does not establish that these visible defects are solved.

Thin-shell rendering remains an acceptable architectural choice; no ray-marching requirement is imposed. The screenshot also confirms the upper hull branding is partially hidden by its mounting geometry; parent has already identified that repair. The side room arrangement and lower brand are clear.

Earth/space cannot receive 8/10 from this image. No numeric overall score is assigned while visual refinements and final responsive/performance validation remain in progress. Separate reading interfaces remain outside this review.
