# Independent v9 acceptance plan

Scope: the seven latest visual and navigation requests, with the reusable database/admin, reading, URL, accessibility, security and maintenance requirements retained. V8 evidence is a regression baseline, not approval of v9. No new score is assigned before the working revision and its evidence are reviewed.

## Required visual and interaction checks

1. **Strong dimming without loss of detail.** Capture one overview with no hover, each room hovered, and keyboard focus on each navigation destination. Nonselected cabins should be substantially darker while floor, wall and fixture forms remain visible; the selected cabin and its physical nameplate must be legible. Leaving hover must restore the same idle appearance. Rapid P→E→A→C switching and dragging over a hotspot must not leave a wrong cabin bright or introduce lighting jumps. Validate active-room and reduced-motion behavior separately. Automated axe cannot assess the rendered canvas contrast.

2. **Plausible open doorways and labels.** Inspect each doorway frontally and obliquely in both model layouts. Prefer square/circular forms, with a rectangular form acceptable if it better serves the scene. Door leaves must be open with a credible recess/hinge/slide pocket, not a perpendicular decorative flap blocking passage. Destination plates should lie flush on the intended wall or jamb, face the visitor without mirrored/back-facing lettering, and avoid floating or passing through trim. Judge surface normals and mounting visually/numerically rather than treating an Euler angle of zero as inherently correct.

3. **An open C-shaped route.** The expected undirected path is Experience↔Projects↔About↔Contact, with the middle connection using the left walkway. This implies six directed side-door routes. There should be no floor or ceiling transfer hatches and no Experience↔Contact shortcut implied by a visible doorway. The left walkway should read as one approximately double-height, half-room-width connector, with dimensions adjusted when needed for a convincing ship. From every cabin, the appropriate left/right opening should reveal useful adjoining architecture rather than an opaque painted door or empty black void. Test both directions of all three edges, correct editable labels, focus and hover illumination, direct room URLs, Back/Forward and stable renderer identity. Confirm overview grid order remains Projects/Experience above About/Contact.

4. **Cloud quality.** Inspect native-size production captures at desktop DPR1, a high-density phone viewport, and zoomed overview framing. Clouds should have continuous soft forms without square texels, repeating coarse cells, noisy shimmer, harsh sheet seams or conspicuous halos. Watch moving samples as well as stills. Record effective DPR, drawing-buffer size, current source version and actual RAF distributions; compare like-for-like with v8, retaining slower samples. A prettier still is insufficient if cloud animation regresses scene responsiveness.

5. **Bottom navigation in interactive view.** The top navbar must be absent visually and from the interactive tab order. The replacement dropdown should be recognizable, touch friendly and reachable by keyboard, identify the current destination, and use editable labels. Test open/close, Enter/Space, Escape, Tab/Shift+Tab, outside click, focus return and selected-link activation; do not impose ARIA menu behavior unless the implementation actually follows that pattern. It must not trigger ship drags or block room controls/readers at 320px, 390px, tablet, desktop, browser zoom or short landscape heights. Include safe-area inset handling. Reading mode, no-JS pages, loading failure and WebGL-loss fallback must still expose complete semantic navigation and the motion-hiding escape. Switching view or routes must preserve draft preview identity, contact draft/success state and meaningful URL/history.

6. **Remove the bright wall patch.** Capture all four settled rooms and hover states, especially the wall between each pair of lights. The region should have the same believable material response as the surrounding liner, without a blown-out center rectangle or additive highlight masquerading as a lamp. Do not compensate by introducing another unnecessary central source. Check that revised light/material attenuation does not erase physical labels or create black seams, and that cached AO updates during actual geometry/light changes.

7. **Interesting but honest empty bays.** Inspect 0, 1, 5, 8, 9 and 10 published projects. Empty compartments should contain deliberate varied stowed equipment or other coherent spacecraft detail without pretending to be portfolio projects. They must have no stale project title, slug, hit target, focusable button or hover-open behavior. Test publishing/unpublishing, page changes, private preview and wide/compact resize without rebuilding the scene. Restore the original nine sample records and verify database state. Generic fixture labels must also be editable when they are visible copy.

## Source-sensitive regression risks

- V8 declares eight directed portals including up/down hatches in spacecraft-model.ts. Replace the topology consistently in visual objects, pick targets, CSS3D hotspots, label positions, bounds/framing points and audits. Deleting only geometry would leave ghost interaction targets.
- Layout changes must recompute world-space anchors and physical reader matrices. Test a project clipboard and contact panel after resizing in place, plus keyboard focus that previously exposed CSS3D parent scrolling.
- Removing the header changes the safe camera frame. Inspect all header measurement and reserved inset paths, not merely its display CSS. Account for the closed bottom navigation without reintroducing cut-off doors or lockers.
- Preserve the sticky maximum-excursion drag coordinator and its capture-level click suppression for native controls. Native mouse evidence and synthetic touch-handler evidence should remain clearly distinguished; there is no physical-touch claim.
- Keep sample presentation gated by demo/private preview. Do not remove the independent noindex safeguards for fictional records simply to hide badges.

## Final review evidence and scoring

Five equal areas remain: visual identity/integrated interaction; functionality/database/admin; responsive accessibility; performance/crawlability; security/maintainability. Score each from current source and demonstrated behavior, with total equal to twice their sum. Passing requires ≥90/100, each area ≥8/10 and no local requirement blockers. Require fresh clean production images, current all-six doorway circuit, dropdown keyboard/mobile results, real browser mouse-drag regression, reader/contact/history checks, no-JS HTTP and build/asset checks, baseline workflow tests, published-count restoration, dependency audit and current frame measurements.

Physical device touch, manual assistive-technology testing and real-user CWV remain unverified unless new evidence is supplied. The prior remote Sites callback-registration HTTP409 incident remains separate from the requested local revision; no public deployment claim follows from a successful local build.
